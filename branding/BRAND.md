# Arbor Vitae Wellness — brand spec

Brandon's first client: chiropractic clinic in Santa Monica (Dr. Magdy S.
Guirguis, D.C.). Colors taken from the practice site's own stylesheet (ArborVitae_Website
styles.css, read-only): CTA green `--grn-d #2AA30F` with `--grn #38C41A` as the
lighter hover, on their darkest forest `--fst3 #0A2011` for text-on-brand
(5.5:1, AA). Neutral hue 100 reproduces the site's green-tinted grays
(`--lin #E4E7DE`, `--mist #E6EBE4`). Their forest `--fst #1B4A27` is a section
BACKGROUND, not a CTA color — using it as the brand made every button read as
near-black. Logo: tree mark from assets/logo.png.
